How to Play

  • Move: WASD / arrow keys, Shift to sprint. Look: move the mouse (click the game once to capture the mouse; Esc releases it).
  • Cast: hold the LEFT mouse button, draw a spell's glyph in one stroke, release. The spell flies where the crosshair points. There are no turns: draw as fast as you can and spam spells while you have mana.
  • Precision matters: 92%+ is Perfect (fastest, full power, may crit). Down to 78% still casts but is weaker, slower and costs more mana. A dashed ghost shows which glyph the game thinks you are drawing.
  • Mana refills every second. HP slowly refills when nothing has hurt you for 5 seconds. Rest at the Great Hall hearth to refill everything.
  • Enemies glow before they attack. Dragons mark the ground where their breath or dive will land: step out of the red area.
  • Levitate: draw the rising chevrons to float for 2.5 seconds; enemy shots, breath and ground attacks miss you while you float.
  • Inventory I: equip one staff (more damage, boosts to certain spells, some even grant a spell) and one hat (more HP or mana, faster mana, stronger shields, shorter cooldowns). Enemies drop them, quests reward them, and some are hidden (look with Wandlight).
  • Gold & shop: beaten enemies, quests and chests give gold. Talk to Tilly Wrenfeather in the Great Hall to buy staffs, hats and potions, or sell gear. Items show green/red differences against what you wear. Potions: 1 Health, 2 Mana.
  • Weaknesses: nameplates show what an enemy is weak to (e.g. Weak 🔥); in battle a line tells you which of your spells to use.
  • Training Dummy: in the east of the Great Hall. Press E to practise drawing, interrupting and Levitate safely.
  • Map M and minimap (top right): the ★ and the golden light pillar show where your tracked quest wants you to go; the arrow at the top points there. Pick which quest to follow with 📍 Track in the Quest Log.
  • Shrines ✦: touch one (E) to attune it. Then travel to any attuned shrine from a shrine or from the map.
  • Doors glow so you can spot them; usable gates glow in their state colour (gold locked, blue sealed, purple boss).
  • Wandlight: press L (or draw the check-mark) to light your wand. It lights dark places, outlines enemies and reveals hidden runes.
  • Doors: press E to open. Locked doors need a key; sealed doors break when hit by the right spell.
  • Interact: E talks, opens chests, picks up items. Quest Log Q, Spellbook B (practise any glyph there).
  • No mouse capture? Open the game with index.html?nolock: hold LEFT to draw at the cursor, hold RIGHT and drag to look.
  • Touchscreen PC or laptop: touch the screen and draw a glyph with your finger, lift to cast. Keep using the keyboard to move and the mouse to look.
  • Tablet mode / phone: left thumb moves; draw glyphs with a finger on the right side. Tap 👁 Look to drag the view instead, ✋ Draw to switch back.
  • Battles: enemies never attack you on their own. Walk up to one and press E (⚔ Fight) to start a one-on-one battle in an arena. In the arena nobody walks: you and your foes stand your ground and only cast. A Perfect or Great hit while a foe is glowing to attack interrupts it. Win to return to the same spot. Press F (🏃 Flee) to escape, except from bosses and dragons.
  • Practice Duel: you against 1–5 CPU wizards, every spell unlocked, 400 HP, 800 mana.
